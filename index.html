LuckySearch: Your Central Hub

Extensive Customization
Make it truly yours with a deep design system. Change accent colors to match your brand, pick your favorite typography for better legibility, or even rename the search bar to something personal.
Smart Navigation & Multi-Language
Search in Spanish even if Premiere is in English. Results are organized into Collapsible Categories that stay hidden by default to save space, but expand instantly to reveal all your assets.
Surgical Pinning & Hot-Buttons
Mark any tool with a star to build your toolbox. Your favorites and recently used items are transformed into one-tap persistent buttons at the bottom for instant application without typing.
Integrated Curve Preview
Visualize the mathematics of your motion. See your animation curves directly within the search bar before applying them. Precision control without guesswork, keeping your focus entirely on the timeline.
Zero-Lag Performance Engine
Experience search-as-you-type response times. Real-time preset reading and tool indexing with absolutely zero lag. Built for editors who need their tools to move at the speed of thought.
Critical Note on Presets
Due to current Adobe API limitations, custom interpolation curves cannot be preserved during programmatic application. Manual easing adjustments may be needed.

LuckyShortcuts: Turbo Hotkeys

1 Action Per Key Only (NO MACROS)
LuckyShortcuts does NOT support multi-step automation. It only applies one single tool per shortcut to keep your workflow fast and 100% stable.
Direct 1-to-1 Mapping
Assign any preset, transition, or effect directly to your keyboard. Use the built-in search hub to find and map actions in seconds, creating a custom ecosystem at your fingertips.
Global Stability Protocol
Engineered for absolute reliability. By focusing on robust 1-to-1 commands instead of complex macros, we ensure a system that never fails or conflicts with your host.
High-Performance Response
Experience fast execution times. Our optimized engine is designed to minimize the delay between your keypress and the action in Premiere Pro, keeping your creative flow smooth.

LuckyZoom: Surgical Framing

Grow Mode PRIMARY
Grow is a native Premiere Pro effect introduced in recent versions — and it’s a game-changer. It uses Premiere’s own keyframe handles and velocity curves to generate buttery-smooth, mathematically perfect zooms. LuckyZoom harnesses it to the fullest.
Visual Curve Editor: Adjust exactly how your zoom starts and ends. Control Influence In/Out with a real-time preview ball below the graph.
Transform Mode SECONDARY
A lightweight alternative for when you need quick, simple zooms. Transform mode works directly with Premiere’s standard Transform effect — great for fast adjustments. Can be linked to LuckyCurves to apply organic motion, using keyframes to simulate complex curves.
Dynamic Zoom — A→B Framing
Define a start point (A) and an end point (B) anywhere in the frame. LuckyZoom smoothly animates between them — zoom-in, zoom-out, or lateral reframe — with frame-perfect precision every time.
Free Aspect — Deform Mode
Break free from 16:9. Hold Shift while zooming to deform the frame independently on both axes — stretch, squash, or skew your shot without any aspect ratio constraints.
Eagle View
Zoom from a wider perspective of the frame. Eagle View lets you set zoom targets from a pulled-back vantage point — perfect for contextual reframing when you need to see the big picture before locking your shot.
Intelligent Preview Navigation
Navigate your preview exactly like Premiere Pro. Use your mouse scroll wheel to zoom into specific areas for surgical precision. Built-in scroll bars appear when expanded, giving you total control over your framing.
