LuckyTools was born from a simple goal: to create the tools that professional editors actually want to use in their daily high-pressure workflows.

The Story Behind LuckyTools

LuckyTools was created after years of editing for top-tier creators and experiencing the friction of repetitive tasks firsthand. Across the editing community, many editors have already built their own plugins, scripts, and workflow fixes to solve these same problems. That spirit is not new; LuckyTools simply grew from the idea of bringing that energy into one unified place.

What makes LuckyTools unique is its integrated ecosystem. It's not just a set of disconnected plugins; it's a harmonized environment where community-tested tools work in sync to save you hours of manual labor.

The Power of Community

LuckyTools is developed in exclusive alliance with Busco Editor, the largest network of professional video editors in LATAM. Many editors in this community have created their own plugins and shared solutions for years, including tools and options that have been in development for a long time. Our goal is to keep imagining new tools with the community, test them with 9,000+ elite creators, and turn the best ideas into a unified ecosystem for everyone.

Clarifications & Requirements Must Read

Which languages are supported by LuckyTools?

LuckyTools is **exclusively compatible with Adobe Premiere Pro in English or Spanish**. We have prioritized these two languages because they are the most widely used among editors. Supporting additional languages is an extremely heavy task, so we haven't implemented it yet. To ensure the plugin functions correctly, please make sure your Premiere Pro installation is set to either English or Spanish.

Is LuckyTools compatible with macOS?

Currently, LuckyTools is exclusively available for Windows. I do not support macOS at this time. If you are a Mac user, I kindly ask that you refrain from purchasing the plugin, as it will not function on your system. I hope to explore Mac support in the future, but for now, this is a Windows-only toolkit.

Which versions of Adobe Premiere Pro are supported?

I guarantee 100% stable performance only for Adobe Premiere Pro 2026 (v26.0) or higher. While the tools may load on versions below v26, they are prone to significant bugs and instability. I strongly recommend updating your software before purchase.

Does it work perfectly on Windows with Premiere 2026?

Yes! LuckyTools is natively built and optimized for Windows environments. When paired with Premiere Pro 2026, you will experience high feature compatibility, good stability, and a smooth editing workflow.

Are there any bugs or known limitations in the plugin?

LuckyTools is a project that is always growing and evolving. While we do our best to make sure everything is stable, it's possible that you might find some minor errors or quirks along the way.

The best part is that we are constantly listening to your feedback. Your support helps us release regular updates and fixes, making these tools better and more reliable for every editor in the community!

Does this plugin replace other dedicated tools?

It does not intend to. The focus with LuckyTools is to group everyday functions to speed up workflows quickly and easily. Because of this, the tools are straightforward in terms of configuration and do not offer great depth—the goal is to maintain ease of use without overwhelming settings or complex menus.

Ultimately, the true vision behind LuckyTools is to act as a unified ecosystem, where all its panels and tools communicate seamlessly with each other to multiply your efficiency across the entire editing process.

How does the license work? Can I use it on multiple computers?

Each purchase of LuckyTools grants you 1 license per machine. This means the license is tied to the specific computer where you activate and install the plugin. It is not a floating license that works across multiple machines simultaneously.

However, this does not mean you are permanently locked to one computer forever. LemonSqueezy — the platform that handles all purchases — gives you full control over your license directly from your buyer account. You can:

  • Deactivate the license on your current machine at any time.
  • Re-activate it on a new or different computer after deactivating.
  • Manage everything from your LemonSqueezy page using your purchase email — no need to contact support.

So if you change computers, reinstall Windows, or simply want to move the plugin to another machine, the process is straightforward: deactivate your old activation from your LemonSqueezy page and activate on the new device. This gives you full flexibility while keeping the license model fair and sustainable for continued development.

If you ever have trouble managing your license, feel free to reach out through the Contact page and we'll help you out.
